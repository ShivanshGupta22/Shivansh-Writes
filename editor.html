Writing tips

  • 500–800 words is a comfortable length for one article — aim for 3 to 5 sections.
  • Use ## for section headings; they automatically build the table of contents on the article page.
  • Write a real description: it is what people see on cards, in search results and in previews.
  • Add 3 or 4 tags — they power search and the “related articles” list at the bottom of each post.
  • Press Ctrl / Cmd + S to save a draft quickly while writing.
